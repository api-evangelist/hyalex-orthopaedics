Hyalex Orthopaedics, a privately-held medical device company developing transformational cartilage biomimetic products, today announced the appointment of Carl Vause as its new chief executive officer and president. Mr. Vause, who succeeds Hyalex’s interim president, Michael Hawkins, Ph.D., has also been appointed to the company’s board of directors.
